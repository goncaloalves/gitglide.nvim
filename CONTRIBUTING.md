# Contributing to GitGlide.nvim

Thank you for your interest in contributing! All contributions are welcome.

## How to Contribute

1.  **Fork the repository** and clone it to your local machine.
2.  **Create a new branch** for your feature or bug fix: `git checkout -b my-awesome-feature`.
3.  **Make your changes**. Please ensure your code follows the existing style.
4.  **Commit your changes** with a descriptive commit message.
5.  **Push your branch** to your fork: `git push -u origin my-awesome-feature`.
6.  **Open a Pull Request** back to the main repository.

## Reporting Bugs

If you find a bug, please open an issue and provide as much detail as possible, including your Neovim version, plugin configuration, and steps to reproduce the error.
